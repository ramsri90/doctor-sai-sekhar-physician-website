## 2. Step-by-Step External Actions for #1 Visibility

### Step 1: Google Search Console (Instant Google Indexing)
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add your website URL: `https://www.drsaisekharphysician.com`.
3. Select **Sitemaps** from the left menu.
4. Enter `sitemap.xml` and click **Submit**.
5. Go to **URL Inspection**, enter `https://www.drsaisekharphysician.com`, and click **Request Indexing**. (This triggers Google to re-crawl and index the site within 24–48 hours).

### Step 2: Google Business Profile (Google Maps & Knowledge Panel)
When someone searches **"Trinetra Medicals"**, Google shows a Google Maps Knowledge Panel.
1. Open [Google Business Profile Manager](https://business.google.com/).
2. Verify or update the Business Name to: **Trinetra Medicals - Dr. Sai Sekhar P**.
3. In the **Website** field, set: `https://www.drsaisekharphysician.com`.
4. Ensure the phone number matches: `+91 63007 93688`.
5. Address: Beside Abhiruchi Sweets, Ramalayam Street, Muralinagar, Visakhapatnam - 530007.

### Step 3: Bing Webmaster Tools
1. Visit [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).
2. Sign in and select **Import from Google Search Console**.
3. Confirm submission of `https://www.drsaisekharphysician.com/sitemap.xml`.

### Step 4: Verify Schema Markup
1. Open the [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results).
2. Enter `https://www.drsaisekharphysician.com`.
3. Ensure `MedicalClinic`, `Physician`, and `BreadcrumbList` pass with green checkmarks.
